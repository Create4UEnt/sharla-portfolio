How a project runs

Clear steps. No guesswork. You approve every stage.

01

Discovery

A short call or site visit to understand your goals, audience, timeline and where the work will be seen, in person or on camera.

02

Concept

Mood boards, lookbooks or photo-real renders of your actual space, so you can see the result before you commit.

03

Proposal

Tiered options with clear pricing, what's included and a schedule. Pick the level that fits your budget.

04

Produce & deliver

Sourcing, install, filming, editing or build. One creative lead manages it end to end, with check-ins throughout.

Investment
