01 — The Idea

Your building already looks like a firm people can trust. This season, let it glow.

Bailey Law Firm sits in one of the most handsome buildings on the street: a two-story porch, black shutters, cast-iron railings, and a grand staircase up to the front door. It doesn't need a pumpkin patch. What it needs is an entrance that stays symmetrical, restrained and warm, so clients feel welcome before they reach the door.

This proposal covers two seasons on the same design. Fall uses deep burgundy, burnished copper and olive. Holiday moves to cedar, magnolia, brass and red velvet. The warm-white lighting stays up through both seasons, so the switch is quick and your porch is never bare.

Two focal lines

Lit garland follows the upper balcony banister and the first-floor porch railing. It's visible from the street and reads clearly at night.

A framed front door

Matched urns, a restrained wreath and lit handrails guide visitors up the stairs to the door.

Warm white only

Only 2700K candlelight-tone bulbs, with no color or flashing. The lights run on a timer from dusk to 11 pm.

Safe for a historic facade

Nothing is nailed, screwed or taped to wood or iron. Everything comes off cleanly, and every stair tread stays clear.

Care for the building

  • No fasteners. Garland is secured with padded ties and soft wraps. Nothing is nailed, screwed or taped to the siding, columns or cast iron.
  • Clear stairs. Handrails stay fully grippable, and no décor or cords are placed on any tread.
  • Cords off the walking path. All wiring is outdoor-rated and runs along railings and porch edges to your existing exterior outlets, on a timer.
  • Flags and signage untouched. Your door sign and lanterns stay fully visible.

Terms

  • Deposit. 50% at signing, and the balance on the day of installation. For bundles, the holiday balance is due at the holiday swap.
  • Décor stays with Create4Me. All décor and lighting is supplied, maintained, removed and stored by Create4Me. Would you like to keep the fall or holiday pieces? You can purchase them at season's end for 40% of that season's package price.
  • Live plants. Mums and fresh greenery are replaced on care visits if they're damaged by weather.
  • Validity. Founding client pricing is held until October 10, 2026.
