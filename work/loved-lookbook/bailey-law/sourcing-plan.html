    • Measurements are estimated from the photos: stair handrails about 24 ft total, first-floor porch railing about 36 ft (both sides), upper balcony about 50 ft. Garland comes in 6 or 9 ft lengths. Measure on site before ordering; the quantities below are what these lengths need.
    • Lights run about 1.5× the garland length because they wrap through it. Plan on commercial-grade warm-white (2700K) LED minis, 100 bulbs per strand, about 33 ft each.
    • Your own time is not counted as a cost. Profit is what you pay yourself. Helper hours are only for installs and takedowns.
    • Lights, cords, timers and urns are bought once. On a single-season package they're charged to that season. In a bundle they're only counted once, and the holiday install becomes a quicker décor swap.
    • Décor stays with Create4Me, so reusable pieces (marked Keep) become your inventory for next year's clients. That's why year 2+ profit is much higher.
