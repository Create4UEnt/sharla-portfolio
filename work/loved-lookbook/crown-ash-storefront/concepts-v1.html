02 — What the Site Tells Us

The awning is already the color. We build around it.

Wine-red awning + red brick. Stick to wine, bronze, ivory and black. Bright orange would clash with the brick.
Gold-and-cream logo on the door. Brass and cream accents repeat it at eye level.
Brick piers on each side. This is where the planters go, flanking the door symmetrically without blocking the glass.
No step at the door. Glass doors open straight onto the concrete, so the rug has to be thin and the doors must clear it.
Glass reflects everything. Tall shapes and warm lantern light keep the entrance readable against the reflections.

04 — Rules We Designed To

Every option follows the same placement rules.

Walkway

At least 60″ of clear sidewalk left open between the décor and the curb, wide enough for two people or a wheelchair (ADA minimum is 36″). Décor stays in a band about 30″ deep against the storefront.

Doors & exit

The double door is an exit. Nothing goes in the door swing, and there's at least 18″ of clear space beside each door on the pull side. The fire-department sign on the glass stays visible.

Rug safety

Low-profile outdoor flatweave, under ½″ thick, with edges taped to the concrete. We test that both doors open over it before we leave.

Wind & weight

Planters weighted with gravel or bricks. Tier stands placed against the piers with their feet weighted, so nothing can tip onto the walk.

Fire & smoke

No real hay, corn stalks or dried grasses. Faux stems only, kept away from ash urns and smoking areas. Battery LED lanterns only, no open flame and no extension cords.

Landlord

Everything stays within Suite 4's frontage. Nothing drilled, glued or hung on the brick, awning or coach lights, and nothing in the landscape beds. We get written approval from property management first.

Season Plan

Install, maintain, then carry into the holidays.

Week 1

Approve & shop

Client picks a direction and property management approves it in writing. Buy everything in one trip, using the Michaels and Hobby Lobby weekly coupons and sales.

Install

Before opening

About 2 hours. Add gravel to the planters, tape down the rug, test both doors over it, then tape-measure the 60″ walkway.

Weekly

Keep it crisp

The storefront gets direct afternoon sun, so water the mums daily in warm weeks. Pinch off spent blooms and re-tape the rug corners as needed.

Late Nov

Holiday swap

Keep the rug, planters, stands and lanterns. Swap the mums and pumpkins for faux evergreens, red berries and gold ribbon, so most of the fall budget carries into the holidays.
