01 — Overview

A fall entrance that looks like Crown & Ash.

Thank you for the chance to work on your storefront. This proposal covers the three fall concepts we developed for your Suite 4 entrance, what the materials will cost for each, and what Create4Me will do to design, source and install the one you choose.

All three concepts use the same idea. Fall in the Carolinas is tobacco harvest season, so the palette comes from cigar wrapper shades (Claro to Oscuro), with gold for the Crown and white-grey for the Ash. Each concept is a mirrored pair of arrangements on the brick piers, with a solid rug at the door. Each one also includes warm evening lighting, since most guests arrive after dark.

Scope of work

Design

Three custom concepts with photo renders of your storefront, elevations, a placement plan and itemized shopping lists.

Sourcing

Create4Me buys everything on the list, times purchases to store sales, and orders the printed crest bands.

Installation

On-site setup (about 2–3 hours): ballast, planting, bands, fairy lights and uplights on timers, and a taped-down rug, with the doors and walkway tested before we leave.

Walkthrough

A short care guide for your staff covering watering, deadheading and light timers, plus one check-in visit in the first two weeks.

06 — Compliance & Site Review

Planned to meet local code and the property's rules.

Before designing, Create4Me reviewed the requirements that apply to a storefront in a Columbia shopping center. All three concepts were built to meet them, and each one is checked again on site before installation.

City of Columbia & Richland County

  • Exits & fire code. Under the International Fire Code (adopted by South Carolina), nothing blocks the entry doors, their swing, or the path to them. The fire-department sign stays visible.
  • Decorative materials. Only faux leaves and stems. No hay, straw, cornstalks or dried grasses near the entrance or the ash urns.
  • No open flame. All lighting is battery LED on timers. No candles, extension cords or cords across the walkway.
  • Accessibility (ADA). The accessible route needs at least 36″ clear. We keep 60″+ open, and the rug is under ½″ with a taped-down edge.
  • Signage. The crest band is decoration on a free-standing planter, not a sign attached to the building. If the city or landlord sees it as a sign, we'll switch to a plain band.
  • Sidewalk. Everything stays within about 30″ of the storefront on private shopping-center property, so no city encroachment permit should be needed. We'll confirm this before install.

Sparkleberry Crossing

  • Written approval. Create4Me will send property management the render and placement plan, and we won't install until they approve in writing.
  • Lease & center rules. We'll check the décor to be sure it complies with Crown & Ash's lease and any center design or common-area rules.
  • Nothing attached. No drilling, adhesive, hooks or ties on the brick, awning, columns, coach lights or glass.
  • Stable & weighted. Tall planters are ballasted with gravel and pumpkin towers are pinned, for wind and for pressure-washing crews.
  • Neighbors. Everything stays within the Suite 4 frontage and off shared walks and neighboring storefronts.
  • Care during the season. Create4Me checks the arrangement in week 2 and resets anything that has shifted, so the walkway and doors stay clear.
Our commitment

If any requirement from the city or property management changes the design, Create4Me will adjust it at no extra design cost before anything is purchased.
