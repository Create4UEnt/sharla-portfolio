01 · Light

Golden-hour backlight.

Warm, low, consistent direction. Last 45 minutes before sundown. No flash, no fill — only what the sky gives.

02 · Skin

Part of the landscape.

Treat talent as texture among textures — grass, leaves, water, fabric, skin. Not staged against the scene but folded into it.

03 · Palette

Whatever the field is wearing.

Butter, peach, terracotta, sage, oat, cream. Nothing pulls the eye away from her skin under raking light.

Look 01 · Linen
Look nº 01

Oat & ochre.

Linen sets in field tones — she disappears into the grass and reappears in the light.

Outfit
Cropped linen camisole + wide-leg linen trouser in oat. Unbuttoned overshirt (rust or ochre) draped, half-off shoulder. Barefoot or thin leather sandal.
Fabric
Raw, undyed linen; visible weave; slightly wrinkled — never pressed.
Hair & makeup
Natural coils, loose. Skin glossed with face oil for backlight catch. Bare lash, glossed lip, cheek warmth only.
Accessories
Thin gold hoops. One stacked ring. A single stem of wheat or dried grass in hand.

Look 02 · Silk slip
Look nº 02

Silk, skin, sun.

A bias-cut slip so thin the light passes through it — a second skin under golden hour.

Outfit
Champagne, olive or blush silk slip. Bias-cut, mid-calf. Delicate spaghetti strap, cowl neck. Optional silk scarf tied loose at wrist or waist.
Fabric
100% silk charmeuse — semi-sheer when backlit. Slight cling in the breeze.
Hair & makeup
Slicked back low bun or damp, air-dry curls. Dewy skin, flushed cheek, tinted balm on lip.
Accessories
Thin gold ankle chain. Small pearl studs. Bare feet. No bag.

Look 03 · 70s bohemian
Look nº 03

Seventies, sun-faded.

Embroidered cotton, a low-slung flare, the smell of dried flowers and warm dust.

Outfit
Cream embroidered peasant blouse, bell sleeve, drawstring neckline. Low-rise faded denim flare or amber corduroy flare. Vintage suede belt.
Fabric
Sun-bleached cotton with hand-embroidery in rust and sage. Softened denim, faded at the thigh.
Hair & makeup
Middle part, natural curls loose and full. Sun-kissed cheek, bronzed lid, glossed lip. Nothing that reads "makeup."
Accessories
Long silver + turquoise pendant. Silk head scarf tied under the hair. A small bouquet of wildflowers.

Look 04 · Prairie
Look nº 04

A dress made of daylight.

Prairie silhouette, cotton eyelet, the hem catches the wind — cottagecore without the costume.

Outfit
Cream cotton eyelet midi or maxi. Puffed short sleeve or off-shoulder tie. Tiered skirt, hem to mid-calf. Optional soft ribbon sash in butter yellow.
Fabric
Broderie anglaise / eyelet cotton. Featherweight. Slightly translucent when backlit.
Hair & makeup
Half-up with a soft ribbon bow. Baby hairs laid gently. Cheek + lip in the same warm peach. Skin sheer, glowing.
Accessories
Butter-yellow satin ribbon. Small daisy tucked at the ear. Bare feet in grass.

Look 05 · Sheer
Look nº 05

Almost not there.

Sheer whites, tulle, a slip beneath — she becomes light passing through cloth.

Outfit
Sheer white cotton voile blouse over cream silk slip. Long tulle skirt, ivory, layered — moves like water. Or a single sheer white maxi with soft underlayer.
Fabric
Cotton voile + tulle + silk. Three weights of translucency. Everything catches the light differently.
Hair & makeup
Coils pulled back with a wide silk headband, a few pieces loose at the temple. Skin luminous, not glossy. Balm on lip. Nothing on lash.
Accessories
Silk ivory headband. Small gold cross or delicate pendant. Bare arms, bare feet.

Look 06 · Wrap
Look nº 06

By the water, unhurried.

A soft wrap in sage or sand — she wades, the hem darkens, the light forgives everything.

Outfit
Cotton or lightweight rayon wrap dress in sage green or sun-bleached sand. Deep V, tie at waist, midi length. Or a sarong wrapped over a slip.
Fabric
Soft, drapey cotton or rayon. Meant to darken when wet at the hem.
Hair & makeup
Wet-look damp curls or slicked back. Dewy skin, no product on face beyond balm. Water is the styling.
Accessories
Nothing. Maybe a shell on a string. Wet hem allowed. Bare feet in shallow water.
