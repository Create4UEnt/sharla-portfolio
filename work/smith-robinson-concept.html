Day and dusk

Two looks, one installation.

The lighting goes up once in November and stays through the holidays — only the décor changes between seasons.

Design intent

Quiet confidence, warmly lit.

Smith | Robinson brings together more than 150 years of South Carolina practice in Sumter and Camden, so the decor should feel rooted and timeless, never trendy. Southern magnolia leads the design as a nod to home. The building already does a lot: white stucco piers, black steel glazing, bronze sconces and two Mediterranean olive jars. Our job is to add warmth without competing with it. Everything sits on the existing architecture, every light is warm white, and the look stays inclusive so it can run from Thanksgiving well into January.

Frame, don't fill

The decor follows the piers, the canopy and the window grid so the entrance gets more formal, not busier.

One light temperature

Only 2700K warm white, all steady. That matches the sconces you already have and reads as hospitality, not retail.

Seasonal, not sectarian

Evergreen, birch, magnolia and brass. No religious symbols or characters, which suits a client-facing professional firm.

Placement specifications

What goes where

1

Olive jar planters

Keep the boxwood as a base. Set three white birch poles at staggered heights (4', 5', 6') toward the back of each jar, add a full collar of fir tips and magnolia, and let cedar spill over the rim so it softens the jar's shoulder. Weave warm micro-lights through the greenery and up one birch pole.

Quantity
2, mirrored
Power
Battery pack with 6-hour dusk timer, hidden in the soil
Care
Weekly visit to refresh fresh tips and check lights
2

Pier wreaths

A 36" mixed fir and magnolia wreath centered on each white pier about 18" below the sconce, so the sconce's light washes over it at night. Finish with an ivory velvet bow at the bottom and tails that hang to roughly 5 ft.

Size
36" diameter, 8" profile
Mounting
Removable adhesive stucco anchors or a tension band around the pier, with no drilling
Lights
Pre-lit 5mm warm white on battery timer
3

Canopy garland

The hero piece. A 12" diameter lit garland runs straight along the black steel fascia above the doors, with no swags, so it matches the building's horizontal lines. At each end it turns down the inner face of the pier about 3 ft. Lit greenery shows up best against the black.

Length
~22 LF plus 6 LF of returns (site-measure)
Mounting
Concealed clips on the top edge of the fascia
Power
Hardwired to the nearest exterior GFCI on a photocell timer
4

Clerestory lobby tree

The tall glass above the canopy looks into the lobby chandelier. Put a 12' slim noble fir just inside that glass, lit densely in warm white and decorated in ivory and brass. From the street it reads as a lantern above the entrance. Add a light magnolia ring to the chandelier.

Size
12' × 5' slim profile (confirm ceiling height)
Placement
Interior, clear of egress and fire sprinklers
Note
Doubles as the interior lobby display
5

Door wreath pair

A matched pair of 22" wreaths, one centered on each door leaf at eye level, hung on clear monofilament from the transom. They make the doors read as one formal pair and leave the suite signage and push hardware clear.

Size
22" diameter, slim 4" profile so they clear the door swing
Mounting
Monofilament from the transom with clear cups on the inside of the glass
Lights
None, so they don't fight the reflections
6

Vestibule lanterns

On the tiled entry, one oversized matte-black steel lantern on each side of the doors (40" and 30" on each side, grouped) with warm LED pillar candles. Tuck a small cedar and cone base at their feet. They pick up the window frames and keep the floor area tidy.

Size
40" + 30" per side
Clearance
Keep a 44" clear path to the doors (ADA)
Security
Weighted bases with a discreet cable tether
7

Wing window rhythm

Your building is wide, so this is what ties it together. Hang a 24" wreath with an ivory bow centered in every other bay of the black-mullion windows on both wings, all at the same height. The repetition carries the entrance look across the whole front. Final count will come from full-facade photos.

Size
24" diameter
Mounting
Exterior hangers or monofilament from the window head
Lights
Battery micro-lights on the same timer as #2
Lighting plan

Lit like a residence, not a storefront.

Your existing bronze sconces set the tone. We add light in layers: the garland as the main glow, the wreaths lit by the sconces, the planters as soft points, and the lobby tree as a lantern above it all.

2700K warm white onlyMatches the existing sconces. No cool white, color or RGB.
Steady burnNo twinkle, chase or flashing modes.
Commercial-grade LEDRated for wet locations, UL-listed, with coaxial connectors.
Dusk to 11pmPhotocell with an astronomical timer, so it's on when people leave work and at evening events.
GFCI-protected circuitsHardwired items go only on existing exterior outlets. No cords across walkways.
Glare checkNothing points toward the glass doors, so the entry doesn't mirror back at night.
